Rules that now hold across the account

Nobody under 45Every set starts at 45. Two sets stop at 64. The two that keep 65+ carry a men's bidding rule so they do not drift to older women only.
One optimisation eventAll five sets optimise for Purchase on the same Meta dataset, 7-day click and 1-day view, with the server-side tracking chain unchanged.
One Page, one InstagramEvery live ad is published from the main Longevity Life Academy Page and its Instagram account. The 29 ads tied to the old Instagram account were deleted.
Every click is labelledEach ad carries campaign, ad set, ad and placement identifiers in its link, so CRM sales map back to the exact ad set and ad.
Instagram where it earns itInstagram Feed and Reels stay on in the volume set and the Advantage+ set only; the three tighter sets run Facebook Feed and Reels.
Daily checkAge, gender and placement breakdowns reviewed every morning; the Advantage+ set is pulled back the moment it drifts to low-intent inventory.
