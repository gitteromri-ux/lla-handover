Longevity Life Academy · Blueprint · Monday 9 November 2026

Four ad sets became five.
Every one now aims at people who buy.

Until 09:00 today the account bought cheap clicks from the wrong people: mostly women over 65, audiences up to 90 million wide, one ad doing all the work. Since 09:00 every ad set has an intent tier, a 45+ floor, income or buyer-lookalike targeting, men bid up where men convert, and three new films in the sets built for them.

Old strategy vs new strategy

Before · until 9 Nov 09:00

Buy reach, hope for buyers

  • AudienceUp to 15 stacked lists per set, twelve of them under 1,000 people; one set 90–106 million wide because "Engaged Shoppers" sat in an OR with income
  • AgeFrom 25 or 35, open-ended 65+; delivery drifted to women over 65
  • GenderNo bidding rules anywhere
  • PlacementsInstagram Feed on in every set; one set on desktop
  • Budget61% of spend in the two lowest-intent sets (31% + 30%)
  • CreativeOne UGC film carrying every set; Courtney on the wrong Page with no tags; 29 ads linked to the old Instagram
Now · live since 9 Nov 09:00

Rank by intent, pay for intent

  • AudienceFive tiers: paid-buyer lookalike 1% → interests AND top-25% income (24–28M) → top-10% income (10–12M) → 2% lookalikes (3.2–3.8M) → one Advantage+ set seeded with buyers
  • AgeNobody under 45. Two sets stop at 64; the two that keep 65+ carry a men's bid rule
  • GenderMen bid +40% in the volume set, +30% premium, +50% Advantage+ (men started 4 of the last 6 checkouts)
  • PlacementsFacebook Feed + Reels, mobile only; Instagram stays only where it earns it (volume set, Advantage+)
  • Budget51% to the two highest-intent sets (19% + 32%); low-intent tiers cut to 12% each; 25% to Advantage+
  • Creative3 new ads: Man "Five habits at 50", Woman "33 years apart", Julie Gibson Clark 65s. Courtney rebuilt on the main Page. Every ad tagged to ad set and placement. 0 errors

The six levers we pulled

1

Intent tiers

Each set has one job and one audience definition.

15-list stacks → buyer lookalikes, income bands, one Advantage+
2

Age floor 45

Stops the drift to women 65+ who click and never buy.

25/35–65+ → 45–64 or 45+ with men's rule
3

Bid for men

Men start most checkouts; the seed file is 85% women.

no rules → +30% / +40% / +50% where men convert
4

Placements that sold

Reels bargain inventory bought 1,000+ visits and zero carts.

IG everywhere + desktop → FB Feed/Reels mobile, IG in 2 sets
5

Budget follows intent

Money moved from the widest sets to the tightest.

61% low-intent → 51% high-intent, 25% Advantage+
6

New creative, clean tracking

Three films matched to the audience they were made for.

1 ad + broken tags → 3 new ads, every click labelled
