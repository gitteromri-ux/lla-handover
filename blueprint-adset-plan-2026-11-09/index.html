Why #108808 is low intent when its targeting is not "broad". You are right that interests AND (Engaged Shoppers OR top-25% income) is a mid-funnel definition, not broad; #108816 runs the same definition and converts 5.2% of landing-page views. The difference is not the audience, it is what Meta learned to deliver inside it. #108808 has had zero purchases since 28 Sep, so it never left learning-limited; with no conversion to optimise toward, the system falls back to the cheapest responsive inventory in the definition, which here is 65+ women on Reels at $38–75 CPM (95% of its spend). Those are people who tap, read and leave: 921 LPV, 1 add-to-cart, 0 checkouts. A set that cannot find a conversion in 30 days at $59 CPM is not finding buyers, whatever its targeting says. The fix is the signal (optimise on Blueprint checkout so Meta has something to learn from), the age cap (45–65) and taking Reels out, so the cheap-tap pocket is no longer available to it.
